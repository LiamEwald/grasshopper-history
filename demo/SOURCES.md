# Sources for the demo

The demo (`demo/run.py`) is a reconstruction in the manner of the Allianz Arena, Munich
(Herzog & de Meuron with ArupSport, 2002-2005). It is not the architects' design process: the
three authors are fictional, the steps between the milestones below are invented, and every
dimension not listed here is an estimate.

## What the demo follows

| Fact | Used in the demo as | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Herzog & de Meuron chosen 8 Feb 2002; two of eight designs went to a second round (H&dM, von Gerkan Marg) | the competition phase, and an invented "open bowl" study as the alternative to a closed envelope | FC Bayern timeline; Domus, Feb 2002 |
| Planned capacity 66,000 | the first bowl is tuned to about 66,000 seats | Domus, Feb 2002; Arup project page |
| Three-tier bowl; first row lifted so the lower tier is steeper than usual | three tiers; the "First row raised" version | ArupSport via e-architect / Divisare |
| 106 hospitality boxes | `boxes_n = 106` | FC Bayern |
| ETFE cushions: 2,760 / 2,816 / 2,874 depending on the source; 96 diagonal lines and 30 ring transom lines (9 facade, 21 roof) | 96, then 192 lines around; 9 facade and 21 roof rings | Wikipedia; TensiNet |
| Roof: 96 radial steel trusses cantilevering 50 m (another source: 60 m), stiffened by ring purlins | 96 trusses, 50 m, 21 purlin rings | TensiNet; Maurer bearings document |
| Maximum height about 40 m; facade about 12 m to 44 m above the pitch; plan about 260 × 230 m | rim at +40 m, envelope from +12 m, superellipse 260 × 230 m | TensiNet; Dlubal reference |
| About 20 air-conditioning systems | 20 air-handling units | TensiNet |
| Esplanade from the U-Bahn over four four-storey car parks (9,800 spaces) | the esplanade only; the car parks are left out | Wikipedia; Structurae |
| About 2,000 upper-tier seats added (2012); expansion to 75,024 (2014) with upper-tier and north/south curve places | the upper-tier rows and corner changes after opening | Wikipedia |
| LED facade, 2015: about 300,000 LEDs | the LED strips version | FC Bayern; LEDinside |

## What is invented

- The authors Liam, Maya and Jonas and who did what.
- All intermediate steps, studies, restores and the questions in combine summaries.
- The massing studies, C-values per tier, row counts, member sizes, truss depths, the bowl
  plan, concourses, team rooms, the duct ring and floodlight counts.
